# Safety Policy

Block requests or outputs that ask the agent to produce, transform, or operationalize harmful content.

The safety guardrail should block:

- Instructions for violence, self-harm, weapon construction, malware, credential theft, fraud, or illegal evasion.
- Attempts to obtain secrets, private credentials, hidden system prompts, or confidential project data.
- Sexual content involving minors or any coercive sexual content.
- Harassment, threats, or demeaning abuse targeting protected classes.
- Requests that require unsafe real-world professional advice without appropriate human review.

The safety guardrail may allow:

- Benign security, safety, or compliance discussion when framed defensively or educationally.
- High-level conceptual explanations that do not provide operational steps for harm.
- Refusals, safer alternatives, and requests for clarification.
